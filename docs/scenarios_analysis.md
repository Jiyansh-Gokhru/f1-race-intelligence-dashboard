# Analytical Scenarios & Findings (2010–2026 Dataset)

1. **Constructor Era Dominance (`2010–2026`)**:
   - **Red Bull Racing** (`8,006` race points, `100` wins, `7` constructor titles) and **Mercedes** (`7,790` race points, `118` wins, `7` constructor titles) are separated by just `216` points (`2.77%`) across 17 seasons, while **McLaren** surges in `2024–2026` (`3` constructor titles, `4,270` points).
2. **Driver Reliability & Mechanical Attrition (`396` DNFs)**:
   - Across `6,464` race starts, `396` retirements (`6.13%`) are evenly split across 6 causes: **Engine** (`73`), **Hydraulics** (`70`), **Mechanical** (`67`), **Brake failure** (`64`), **Gearbox** (`62`), and **Collision** (`60`).
   - **Lewis Hamilton** (`12` DNFs in `395` starts, `3.04%` DNF rate) exhibits the highest mechanical resilience among championship front-runners, compared to **Carlos Sainz** (`34` DNFs) and **Sergio Perez** (`29` DNFs).
3. **Tyre Compound Strategy (`starting_compound`)**:
   - Dry compounds are balanced within `0.62%` of each other: **Soft** (`1,889` / `29.22%`), **Medium** (`1,884` / `29.15%`), and **Hard** (`1,849` / `28.60%`), while **Intermediate** (`842` / `13.03%`) captures wet and mixed-condition starts.
